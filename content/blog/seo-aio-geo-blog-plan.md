# Cowinmagnet SEO / GEO / AIO Blog Plan

## Research Basis

Target buyers for Cowinmagnet include importers, distributors, mining companies, recycling plants, cement plants, coal handling plants, EPC contractors and OEM equipment manufacturers. Their search intent is usually not entertainment or broad education. They search because they need to protect equipment, recover ferrous metal, select a suitable separator, compare permanent and electromagnetic designs, or prepare RFQ details.

For Google SEO and AI search visibility, the strongest content angles are practical decision guides with clear definitions, comparison tables, application scenarios, parameter explanations, FAQs and sourcing checklists. These formats are easy for buyers to scan and easy for AI systems to summarize.

Google's public guidance for AI features emphasizes helpful, reliable, people-first content that is crawlable and indexable. Structured data can help search engines understand content, but it should match visible page content. FAQ sections remain useful for users and AI-style answers, even when FAQ rich results are limited in traditional search.

## Publishing Priority

1. **How to Choose an Overband Magnetic Separator for Conveyor Belt Systems**  
   Highest priority because it captures buyers who are close to RFQ and naturally asks for conveyor width, belt speed, material layer depth and iron size.

2. **Permanent vs Electromagnetic Separator**  
   Second priority because it supports product category comparison and internal linking between permanent and electromagnetic pages.

3. **Magnetic Separators for Waste Recycling Lines**  
   Third priority because it builds application authority and can later become an industry landing page for ads and SEO.

## Internal Link Plan

- Link all three posts to `/products/permanent-overband-magnetic-separator`
- Link article CTAs to `/inquiry`
- Link comparison article to future permanent separator and electromagnetic separator category pages
- Link waste recycling article to future `/applications/waste-recycling`

## Schema Recommendations

- Article schema for all posts
- BreadcrumbList schema
- Product links with Product schema on product pages, not necessarily inside blog pages
- FAQ content visible on page; FAQPage schema only if it meets Google's eligibility and is not used in a misleading way
