Buyer questions

FAQ for Faster Decision-Making

Should I choose manual cleaning or self-cleaning?

Manual cleaning is suitable for low iron content. Self-cleaning is better for continuous lines or frequent iron contamination.

Can the magnetic separator be customized?

Yes. Magnetic strength, equipment size, installation method, belt width, color, logo and packaging can be coordinated according to project requirements.

What data should I send before quotation?

Please provide material type, conveyor width, belt speed, material layer depth, iron size, installation height, photos or drawings if available.
