# SEO And GEO

Required checks:

- Per-page title and description.
- One H1 per important page.
- Canonical URLs.
- hreflang plus `x-default` for localized pages.
- `sitemap.xml`, `news-sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, RSS/feed routes.
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata.
- JSON-LD: Organization, Product, Article, Breadcrumb, FAQ where applicable.
- Image alt text for content/product images.

AI search/GEO rules:

- Use clear product names, applications, parameters, and buyer-oriented facts.
- Keep source attribution on generated news.
- Avoid unsupported claims, fake parameters, fake case studies, or invented certifications.
- Prefer structured sections and factual FAQ content.

Generated news must be commentary/summary with source links, not copied third-party articles.
