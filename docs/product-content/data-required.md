# Product Technical Data Required

The current static product records preserve names, model references and product media, but they do not provide enough verified technical data to publish numeric parameters safely across the catalog.

## Required before publishing a numeric specification

For each product, attach one current COWIN source-of-truth item to the relevant CMS record:

- Approved product datasheet or drawing
- Current supplier technical confirmation
- Approved inspection record
- Customer-provided process sheet, where the value is site-specific

## Family queues

- Suspended/self-cleaning: belt width and speed, material layer, suspension height, tramp-iron size, cleaning arrangement, power/cooling where applicable.
- Mineral processing: mineral, feed size and condition, throughput, separation target, product streams, slurry or water requirement.
- Filters: material flow type, temperature, viscosity/flowability, connection, flow rate, cleaning and contact-material requirement.
- Recycling sorting: feed size and mix, upstream ferrous removal, throughput, target fraction and downstream arrangement.
- Metal detection: opening size, product effect, detection target, belt speed and response interface.
- Control/explosion-related equipment: connected equipment, supply, control/interlock design, environment and verified documentation.
- Lifting equipment: load shape, surface condition, handling duty, supply/control, rigging and approved site safety procedure.

## Media queue

Only the main product image is displayed by the new detail template. Extra legacy gallery media is intentionally withheld until it has been reviewed for correct model, COWIN ownership or permission, absence of third-party logos, and absence of QR codes or unrelated information.

## URL decision queue

The `CQZ` and `DCZ` `separator/separation` URL pairs are only flagged as possible duplicates. Confirm the exact model, image, parameters and historical traffic before creating a redirect or canonical change.
